Hoarde(r) privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Hoarde(r) is a game made by Lewis Jaenke. This page explains what the game does with information about you. In short: it doesn't collect any personal information.

What the game stores

Your saved game, best scores and settings (such as volume) are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Things we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or email addresses.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Co-op play

If you choose to play co-op, your device connects directly to your friend's device using a peer-to-peer connection. To set this up, the game uses the free PeerJS connection service, which briefly sees a random room code and your device's network address. Game data such as positions and actions is sent directly between the two devices and is not stored by us. If you never use co-op, no connection is made.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
